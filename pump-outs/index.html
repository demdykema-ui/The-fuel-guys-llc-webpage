The Basics

What Is a Pump-Out?

A pump-out is the process of safely removing diesel, gasoline, or contaminated fuel from tanks, equipment, or vehicles. Whether you have a 50-gallon generator tank or a 10,000-gallon underground storage tank, the fuel needs to come out safely, without spills, and in full compliance with environmental regulations.

This is not a DIY job. Improper fuel removal risks spills, environmental contamination, fines, and equipment damage. Professional pump-out services ensure the fuel is extracted, contained, and disposed of or re-used properly — with full documentation for your records.

Why Hire Professionals?

Environmental regulations, spill prevention requirements, proper disposal protocols, and the specialized equipment needed to do the job right. One mistake can mean costly fines and cleanup. We eliminate that risk.

Common Reasons for a Pump-Out

  • Contaminated fuel — water, algae, sediment, or degradation
  • Wrong fuel type — gasoline in a diesel tank (or vice versa)
  • Tank decommissioning — closing a site or replacing a tank
  • Equipment maintenance — draining before repair or service
  • Seasonal storage — removing fuel before winter shutdown
  • Fuel transfers — moving fuel between tanks or containers
