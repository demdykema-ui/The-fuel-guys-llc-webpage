DEF Supply

Stay compliant, stay running

Diesel Exhaust Fluid is essential for every modern diesel engine equipped with Selective Catalytic Reduction (SCR) technology. Running low on DEF doesn't just trigger warning lights — it can derate your engine, reduce power, and ultimately shut your equipment down entirely. That means lost time, lost revenue, and frustrated crews.

The Fuel Guys delivers DEF in the quantities you need, on a schedule that keeps your tanks full and your engines performing at peak efficiency. We supply DEF in totes, drums, and bulk volumes, and we can coordinate DEF deliveries alongside your diesel fills for maximum convenience.

Our DEF meets all ISO 22241 standards and API certification requirements, ensuring full compliance with EPA emissions regulations. Whether you have two trucks or two hundred, we keep you topped off and compliant without you having to think about it.

  • ISO 22241 and API-certified DEF
  • Totes, drums, and bulk delivery options
  • Scheduled deliveries to prevent run-outs
  • Coordinate with diesel fills for one-stop service
  • Full EPA emissions compliance
  • Prevent engine derating and costly downtime
