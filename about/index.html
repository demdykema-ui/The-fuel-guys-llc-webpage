About The Fuel Guys

Built by contractors. Never dry.

Our Story

The Fuel Guys LLC was founded on a bold idea — that the fuel industry deserves better. Before we started delivering diesel, we were contractors ourselves. We ran job sites, managed fleets, and dealt with the frustration of unreliable fuel vendors who showed up late, overcharged, and couldn't be reached when it mattered most. We built The Fuel Guys to be the company we always wished we could call.

Our mission is simple: deliver fuel on time, at a fair price, with the kind of service that makes our customers' lives easier — not harder. We believe every business, from a one-truck operation to a multi-site construction firm, deserves a fuel partner that treats their schedule and their budget with respect.

We're not just a delivery company — we're a fuel solutions company that innovates at every level, from logistics to customer experience.

What makes us different? We answer the phone. We show up when we say we will. Our invoices match our quotes. And we never charge hidden fees or surprise minimums. In an industry where those basics are rare, we've made them our standard.

We serve businesses across the Denver metropolitan area with dependable, professional fuel delivery. Our experienced team brings fresh thinking, transparent pricing, and a commitment to doing the job right — every single time. We're proud to offer fully bilingual service in English and Spanish, because clear communication is the foundation of great service.

From our founding, we've operated on the principle that our customers' success is our success. When your equipment runs and your crews stay productive, that's a win for everyone. That's what gets us out of bed every morning — making sure nobody runs dry.
